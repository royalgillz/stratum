STRATUM

A warning before the power is cut, and word that your family is safe, with no network.

In the Hollow, a city underground, the Directorate holds the breakers and the network. The network is down, and power cuts come with no warning. STRATUM is a phone app that hears the grid weaken before a power cut, and passes short family messages by sound. No network needed.
