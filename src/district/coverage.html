        Results against us SIMULATED

        • Lamps cannot feed a level: one person's food-floor calories need about 210 kWh of electricity a day with lettuce and over 400 kWh with grain.
        • Shift-sharing of sleeping space raises contact rates.
        • Shafts are not the limit on the return to the surface. Surface reception is.
        • Sharing food at the floor lowers everyone's ration while one level is cut off; when supply is below everyone's floor, everyone breaches, later but together.
        • When most surface crews lie, the quorum is confidently wrong. In the stress week STRATUM's verdict is wrong in every round, on all 20 seeds, while the Directorate's fixed verdict happens to be right.
        • In this model staged trials never cost fewer exposure-hours than one direct 30-day trial: a breach is caught in the first hour either way. Their real value, catching harm that shows only after longer exposure, is not modelled.
        • Heat is not held at zero: STRATUM's heat person-hours are above zero in the seven-day week, and when supply is below every life floor (the stress week) Fair Share breaches every floor equally and they rise far higher.
        • A sudden plant trip gives no warning: the hum helps only with slow shortfalls.
