# Light and food adapter specs

Prefix `ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD`. Design: `lightfood-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. For sector `s`, `id = sectorId(s)`, `p` is its people and `w_s` is `weight(world, s)` (ADAPT-POLICY-009). Each adapter records each sector's power served fraction (ADAPT-POLICY-006) at each step with `t mod 3600 = 0`. At a step with `t mod 86400 = 0`, the daily decision runs first, on the samples of `[t − 86400, t)`; that step's hourly sample then belongs to the next day. For a decision at `t`, `f_s` is the mean of sector `s`'s samples of `[t − 86400, t)`. A sector with `w_s = 0` takes part in no rotation. A sector "eats less than the floor" when it eats less than `FOOD_FLOOR × p × (1 − 1e-9)`.

- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-001** [11]: Under the Directorate, at each step with `t mod 86400 = 0` and `t > 0`, `p11` shall give each sector, for the day just ended, `h = LAMP_H × f_s` bright hours per person and `g = min(p, growSessions(f_s))` farm workers under the grow lamps, count `p` people as meeting the threshold when `h ≥ BRIGHT_H` and `g` otherwise, and set `alloc.lampHours[id]` to `h × p` and `lightDose` to `(g × dose(min(WAKE_H, h + BRIGHT_H)) + (p − g) × dose(h)) / p`.
- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-002** [11]: Under STRATUM, at each such step and for each sector in order, `p11` shall compute `n = sessions(lampKWh(p) × f_s) + growSessions(f_s)`, give sessions to the winners of `rotate(floor(n / w_s + 1e-9), claims, rng)` with one claim per tracked household of the sector (its id, floor 0, demand its members, its debt, starting at 0), store debts from `nextDebt`, count `min(p, w_s × winners' members)` people as meeting the threshold (0 when `w_s = 0`), and set `alloc.lampHours[id]` to `n × BRIGHT_H` and `lightDose` to `share × dose(BRIGHT_H) + (1 − share) × dose(0)` with `share` that count over `p`.
- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-003** [11]: `p11`'s metric shall be named "share of person-days with 4 h of daytime light", unit `share`: people meeting the threshold over all people, summed over sectors and completed days, and 0 before the first day ends.
- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-004** [12]: On its first step `p12` shall set each sector's `foodKcal` to `STOCK_DAYS × FOOD_SUPPLY × p`, and at each `t mod 3600 = 0` shall add `grown(GROW_KW × GROW_LAMP_H / 24 × f, crop)` to the sector's grown food, with `f` that step's power served fraction and `crop` equal to `STAPLE` under the Directorate and `bestCrop()` under STRATUM.
- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-005** [12]: At each step with `t mod 86400 = 0`, `t = 0` included, `p12` shall take each sector's arrivals as that step's `alloc.food[id]` (the day's delivery) plus its grown food since the previous such step (then reset to 0), and under the Directorate let each sector eat `min(FOOD_SUPPLY × p, foodKcal + arrivals)` and keep the rest in `foodKcal`.
- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-006** [12]: Under STRATUM, at each such step `p12` shall let each sector eat `e = min(FOOD_SUPPLY × p, arrivals)`, add the rest of its arrivals to `foodKcal`, then eat `min(foodKcal, max(0, FOOD_FLOOR × p − e))` more from its store.
- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-007** [12]: `p12`'s metric shall be named "days the food floor held after a farm cut-off", unit `days`: with `c` the earliest daily decision `t` at which some `farm-cut` event covers `t` (`atH × 3600 ≤ t < (atH + durH) × 3600`: the first missed delivery) and `b` the first daily decision at or after `c` in which a sector eats less than the floor, the value `(min(world.t, b) − c) / 86400` once `world.t ≥ c`, and 0 before `c` or when no `farm-cut` event covers a daily decision.
- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-008** [11, 12]: `p11` shall declare reads `sectors`, `alloc`, `households` and writes `sectors`, `alloc`, changing only `lightDose` and `alloc.lampHours`, and on its first step shall set `alloc.lampHours` to 0 for `s00` to `s23`; `p12` shall declare reads `sectors`, `alloc` and writes `sectors`, changing only `foodKcal`.
- [x] **ADAPT-LIGHTFOOD-009** [11, 12]: Given the same policy, per-sector power and deliveries, `p11` under the Directorate and `p12` under either policy shall draw nothing, and every `foodKcal` shall stay at or above 0.
