# Family adapter specs

Prefix `ADAPT-FAMILY`. Design: `family-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. A message's destination is `Household.sector` of the household whose id equals `heard.keySet`; its origin is `(heard.payload.level − 1) × 4 + heard.payload.sector`. The network is down at a time `x` when a `network-down` event has `x` in `[atH × 3600, (atH + durH) × 3600)`.

- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-001** [7, 8]: On each step, `p07` shall remove every message from `mesh.inbox` (those left by earlier steps, since it runs before `p08`), drop those without `heard` or without a matching household, and append to `mesh.delivered`, unchanged, those labelled LIVE and those whose `at` falls while the network is up.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-002** [7, 8]: Under both policies, `p07` shall hold each simulated message sent while the network is down that it has not delivered (under STRATUM, those the hop simulation did not deliver), deliver every held message in the step that holds 06:00 with `at` set to that 06:00, and deliver every message still held in the first step whose `t` is outside every network-down interval, with `at` set to that `t`.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-003** [7, 8]: Under STRATUM, for a simulated message sent while the network is down, `p07` shall use one relay per sector node from `createRelay`, built on its first step with the adapter's stream, register the message bytes at the origin with `own`, and transmit those bytes from the origin at the message's `at`.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-004** [7, 8]: For each transmission of bytes `tx.bytes` at `T` seconds, `p07` shall draw once per neighbour along a passable edge, in edge order, and with probability `P_HOP` have that neighbour call `hear(tx.bytes, { reject: 'unknown-tag' }, { state: 'NO_HUM' }, (T + AIR) × 1000)`, schedule a returned `send` as a transmission of its `send` bytes at `atMs / 1000` seconds, remove the pending transmission named by a `cancel`, and process all events in time order and ties in scheduling order.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-005** [7, 8]: `p07` shall deliver a simulated message with `at` equal to the first time its destination node hears it, which may lie up to one step after `world.t`, deliver it at once when origin and destination are the same node, and pass it to the bulletin of ADAPT-FAMILY-002 when no events remain undelivered.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-006** [7]: `p07`'s metric shall be named "messages delivered within 60 min", unit `share`: simulated messages delivered no later than `DELIVER_WINDOW` after their original `at`, over all simulated messages taken from the inbox.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-007** [8]: In the step that holds the `separate` event, `p08` shall go through `world.households` in order, skipping the demo household, and for each member 1 to `members − 1` draw exactly four numbers: away when `u < away`, a level at index `floor(u × 5)` among the other five levels in ascending order, a sector `floor(u × 4)`, and a first-beacon delay `u × FIRST_WINDOW`, using the last three only for an away member.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-008** [8]: `p08` shall give each away member a first beacon at the separation time plus its delay and further beacons every `BEACON_EVERY`, and in each step append to `mesh.inbox` every beacon due in `[t, t + dt)`, with `at` its due time, 25 drawn bytes, byte 0 set to version 3 and type OK, byte 16 set to 4, and `heard` holding type OK, the household id, the world minute `floor(at / 60)`, the member, level and sector, and label SIMULATED.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-009** [8]: `p08` shall mark a separated member known once `mesh.delivered` holds a SIMULATED OK of that household and member number with `at` at or after the separation time, reading only the entries added since its previous step.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-010** [8]: `p08`'s metric shall be named "mean hours until status known", unit `h`: the sum over steps since the separation of unknown separated members times `dt / 3600`, over the number of separated members, and 0 before the separation.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-011** [8]: `familyBoard(world)` shall return, for each household and each member with a delivered OK, the latest such message by `at`, with its minute, level, sector and label, in one pass over `mesh.delivered` and without changing the world.
- [ ] **ADAPT-FAMILY-012** [7, 8]: When a message with a LIVE `heard` of type OK for the demo household is placed in `mesh.inbox`, it shall appear on `familyBoard` labelled LIVE after the next step under either policy, and no metric shall change because of it.
