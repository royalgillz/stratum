# Livable adapter specs

Prefix `ADAPT-LIVABLE`. Design: `livable-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. For sector `s`, `id = sectorId(s)`; "powered" means `alloc.power[id] > 0`; `demand` is the sector's cooling demand `coolingDemandMW(level, people)`. Served fractions follow ADAPT-POLICY-006.

- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-001** [3]: `p03` shall set the cooling pool to `COOL_CAP × grid served fraction`, with `COOL_CAP` the sum of every sector's cooling demand, computed on its first step.
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-002** [3]: Under the Directorate, `p03` shall split the cooling pool in proportion to cooling demand among powered sectors, each capped at its demand, giving unpowered sectors 0.
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-003** [3]: Under STRATUM, `p03` shall set `alloc.cooling` to `allocate(pool, claims).shares` with each sector's demand its cooling demand and its floor `REFUGE` times that demand.
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-004** [1, 3, 9]: On each step, `p03` shall set each sector's `temp` by `tempStep` with the main-area cooling fraction `c`, `wetBulb` by `wetBulb(temp, RH)`, and `co2` by `co2Step` with the ventilation served fraction, where `c` is the served fraction of `alloc.cooling[id]` over `demand` under the Directorate, and under STRATUM the served fraction of `max(0, alloc.cooling[id] − refugePaid)` over `(1 − REFUGE) × demand`, with `refugePaid = min(alloc.cooling[id], REFUGE × demand)`.
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-005** [3]: `p03`'s metric shall be named "person-hours above 27.5 C wet-bulb", unit `person-h`, adding for each step and each sector with `wetBulb > 27.5` its people times `dt / 3600`, times `1 − refugePaid / demand` under STRATUM (1 when the demand is 0).
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-006** [5]: `p05` shall set the pump pool to `24 × PUMP_KW / 1000 × grid served fraction` MW and, under the Directorate, split it in proportion to pump demand among powered sectors, each capped at its demand.
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-007** [5]: Under STRATUM, `p05` shall set `alloc.pumps` to `allocate(pool, claims).shares` with each sector's demand `PUMP_KW / 1000` MW and floor equal to that demand when `strandedIfLost(world.tunnels.edges, flooded, people, HUB)` is above 0, and 0 otherwise, using the flooded flags held at the start of the step, before any sump update.
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-008** [5]: On each step, `p05` shall set each sector's `sumpLevel` by `sumpStep` with `pumpFrac` the served fraction of `alloc.pumps[id]` over `PUMP_KW / 1000` and the script's flood inflow at `t`, update each sector's flooded state by `isFlooded`, and set each tunnel edge passable exactly when neither end is flooded.
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-009** [5]: `p05`'s metric shall be named "stranded person-hours", unit `person-h`, adding for each step the people of every sector with no passable path to the hub (everyone when the hub is flooded) times `dt / 3600`.
- [ ] **ADAPT-LIVABLE-010** [3, 5]: Under STRATUM, while the cooling pool covers every refuge floor and the pump pool covers every critical floor, no sector shall receive less than its floor of either.
