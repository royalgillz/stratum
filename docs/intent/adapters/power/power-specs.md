# Power adapter specs

Prefix `ADAPT-POWER`. Design: `power-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. Sector `s`'s power is `alloc.power[sectorId(s)]` and its demand `powerDemand(sectors[s].people)`; "fully served" means power at least demand × (1 − 1e-9). `DEMAND_MW` is the sum of every sector's power demand. "Capacity" is `capacityAt(script events, t, CAP_MARGIN × DEMAND_MW)`. `D` is only the grid model's load damping.

- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-001** [1]: On each step, `p01` shall set `grid.f` and `grid.rocof` from `swing(grid.f, cap, load, dt)`, `grid.tier` from `tierOf(f, grid.tier)` and `grid.gapMW` to `cap − load`, where `cap` is the capacity at `t` and `load` is the sum of `alloc.power`.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-002** [1]: When a sector that was fully served in the previous step's allocation is not fully served in the current one, `p01` shall record one cut event at time `t − dt`.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-003** [1]: Under STRATUM, `p01` shall count a cut event as warned exactly when the tier has been AMBER or RED at every step from `cut time − WARN_LEAD` to the cut time, counting steps before `t = 0` as GREEN; under the Directorate it shall count no cut event as warned.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-004** [1]: `p01`'s metric shall be named "cuts warned 10 min ahead", unit `share`, value warned cut events over all cut events.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-005** [1, 2]: `p02` shall start shedding in a step where `grid.f ≤ F0 − SHED_HZ`, stop shedding in a step where capacity is at least `DEMAND_MW × (1 + RESERVE)` (if both hold in one step, shedding stops), and while not shedding give every sector its full power demand.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-006** [2]: Under the Directorate, while shedding, `p02` shall start from the sectors served in the previous step, cut whole sectors in the order level 6 to 1 and, within a level, sector 3 to 0 until the served load is at most `capacity / (1 + RESERVE)`, then restore cut sectors in the reverse order whenever a whole sector's demand fits what remains; a cut sector that does not fit is skipped and later sectors are still tried.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-007** [2]: Under STRATUM, while shedding, `p02` shall set `alloc.power` to `allocate(capacity / (1 + RESERVE), claims).shares` with each sector's floor `lifeFloor` and demand `powerDemand`, so no sector falls below its life floor while that supply covers every floor.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-008** [2]: `p02` shall set water supply to `RECYCLE_LPD × total people × grid served fraction` L/day and split it, under the Directorate, in proportion to demand among sectors with power above 0, and under STRATUM by `allocate(...).shares` with per-person floor `WATER_FLOOR` and demand `WATER_DEMAND`.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-009** [2]: `p02` shall set food supply to `FOOD_SUPPLY × total people` kcal/day and split it, under the Directorate, in proportion to demand, and under STRATUM by `allocate(...).shares` with per-person floor `FOOD_FLOOR` and demand `FOOD_DEMAND`.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-010** [2]: `p02`'s metric shall be named "worst-decile served fraction", unit `share`: per sector, the mean over power, water and food of cumulative granted over cumulative demanded; then the person-weighted mean over the sectors with the lowest scores holding exactly 10% of people, taking part of the last sector needed.
- [x] **ADAPT-POWER-011** [1, 2]: When a trip drives `grid.f` from GREEN to at or below `F0 − SHED_HZ` within one step, the resulting cut events shall be recorded and none shall be warned under either policy.
