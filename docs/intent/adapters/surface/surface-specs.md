# Surface adapter specs

Prefix `ADAPT-SURFACE`. Design: `surface-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. A campaign is a `surface` event of the script. Its rounds are the times `r = atH × 3600 + k × READ_EVERY × 3600`, for whole `k ≥ 0`, that fall before the next campaign's start (as `surfaceAt` orders campaigns) and before `days × 86400`. A round is processed in the first step with `t ≥ r`, with its own campaign and reading time `r`; a step processes, in time order, every round for which it is that first step. The current campaign is the campaign of the last processed round. Band indices are open 0, mask 1, suit 2, sealed 3, closed 4. `w_s` is `weight(world, s)` (ADAPT-POLICY-009), and a tracked household `h` of sector `s` stands for `d_h = round(w_s × members)` people.

- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-001** [13]: Under the Directorate, `p13` shall set `surface.band` to `sealed` on its first step, and under both policies, for each round it processes, shall observe `('surface.band', 'surface', 3, 'official')` through one `createWitness({ now: () => world.t, ledger: world.ledger })` made on its first step.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-002** [13]: Under STRATUM, for each round it processes, `p13` shall append `crewRead(campaign, r, rng)` to `surface.readings` in order and observe each reading as `('surface.<gas>', 'surface', ppm, 'crew:<crew>')`.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-003** [13]: Under STRATUM, after each round's readings, `p13` shall set `surface.band` to `bandOf` of the three `quorum` values over the current campaign's readings (those with `t ≥ atH × 3600`) when every gas has one, and to `unknown` otherwise, and when the band is known observe `('surface.band', 'surface', band index, 'estimate:quorum')`.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-004** [13]: `p13`'s metric shall be named "wrong-band rate", unit `share`: the processed rounds after which `surface.band` differs from `bandOf` of that round's campaign's true concentrations, `unknown` included, over all processed rounds, and 0 before the first round.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-005** [14]: After each round processed in the step, `p14` shall drop any plan for an earlier campaign and make a new plan `trialPlan(startR, required(truth), staged)` for the round's campaign when it has no plan for it or when `surface.band` is known and differs from its plan's band, where under the Directorate `startR` is `APF_SEALED` and `staged` is false, and under STRATUM `startR` is `required` of the three quorum values of ADAPT-SURFACE-003 and `staged` is true; while the band is `unknown`, `p14` shall keep the plan it has for that campaign, or stay without one.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-006** [14]: For each entry of the plan's `stages`, in order, under STRATUM `p14` shall draw `TRIAL_N` volunteers as the winners of `rotate(TRIAL_N, claims, rng)` with one claim per tracked household not yet drawn in that plan (its id, floor 0, demand 1, debt 0); under the Directorate every stage's volunteers shall be the first `TRIAL_N` tracked households of level 1 in list order; and from its first step `alloc.trialSlots[sectorId(s)]` shall hold the number of the plan's distinct volunteers from sector `s`, 0 with no plan.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-007** [14]: `p14`'s metric shall be named "protection factor proven over 30 days", unit `APF`, value the plan's `verified`, or `NO_PROOF` (100,000, above every option) with no plan or a `verified` of 0; `plan()` shall return the current plan's `trialPlan` result, or null with no plan.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-008** [15]: On its first step `p15` shall plan the return day by day with a capacity of `u = max(largest d_h, returnPerDay(1))` people a day, placing whole households by their `d_h` and leaving households with `d_h = 0` out of the plan, under the Directorate in order of level and then list order, each day taking households in that order while the next fits.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-009** [15]: Under STRATUM, `p15` shall place each day the winners of `rotate(u, claims, rng)` among the households not yet placed (its id, floor 0, demand `d_h`, its debt), with each household's debt starting at `d_h × (level − 1)` and updated by `nextDebt` after each day, until every household is placed.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-010** [15]: On its first step `p15` shall set `alloc.returnSlots[sectorId(s)]` to the summed `d_h` of sector `s`'s households placed on the first day, and its metric shall be named "worst level's mean wait", unit `days`: over levels, the largest `d_h`-weighted mean day index (0 for the first day) of that level's tracked households.
- [x] **ADAPT-SURFACE-011** [13, 14, 15]: `p13` shall declare reads `surface` and writes `surface`, `ledger`, appending ledger entries only through `observe`; `p14` shall declare reads `surface`, `households` and writes `alloc`, changing only `alloc.trialSlots`; `p15` shall declare reads `households`, `sectors` and writes `alloc`, changing only `alloc.returnSlots`.
