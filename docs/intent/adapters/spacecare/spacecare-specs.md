# Space and care adapter specs

Prefix `ADAPT-SPACECARE`. Design: `spacecare-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. "Tracked households of sector `s`" are the entries of `world.households` with `sector` `s`, in list order; `w_s` is `weight(world, s)` (ADAPT-POLICY-009). "Down" and the 06:00 bulletin are as in ADAPT-FAMILY-002. Courier indices: under the Directorate 0 to `COURIERS − 1`, all at the hub; under STRATUM 0 at the hub and 1 to `POPUP` at the pop-up points. At a step with `t mod 86400 = 0`, `p10` first places the pop-up points, then decides arrivals, then dispatches.

- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-001** [6]: In each step with `t mod 86400 = 0`, `t = 0` included, `p06` shall set each sector's `sleepM2`, under the Directorate to `sleepM2(AREA_M2, people)` with `households[].shift` unchanged, and under STRATUM to `sleepM2(AREA_M2, m)` with `m` the largest entry of `shiftSleepers` for that sector's tracked households and `w_s`, or to `sleepM2(AREA_M2, people)` when the sector has no tracked households.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-002** [6]: Under STRATUM, at each such step and for each sector in order, `p06` shall give shift 0 to the winners of `rotate(floor(Σ tracked members / SHIFTS), claims, rng)` with one claim per tracked household (its id, floor 0, demand its members, its debt, starting at 0), place the other households on shifts 1 and 2 by `fill(their members in household order, [0, 0, 0])`, store each household's debt from `nextDebt`, and change no household field but `shift`.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-003** [6]: `p06`'s metric shall be named "sleeping m2 per sleeper (busiest shift)", unit `m2`, value the person-weighted mean of `sectors[].sleepM2`.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-004** [6]: `census(world, policy)` shall return one `{ sector, shift, asleep, idleM2, sharing }` per sector and shift, in sector then shift order, with `asleep` the people asleep by `shiftSleepers`, at most `people`, `idleM2` the empty assigned floor `idleM2(AREA_M2, people, asleep)`, and `sharing` the households sharing a sleeping space per day (1 under the Directorate, the number of shifts with sleepers under STRATUM), without changing the world.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-005** [10]: On its first step and before any other draw, `p10` shall draw every request of the run with `careRequests(script.events, rng)`, and shall treat a request as sent in the step whose `[t, t + dt)` holds its time.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-006** [10]: `p10` shall make a request sent while the network is up arrive at its own time, and one sent while it is down arrive at the earlier of the next 06:00 at or after its time and the end of that network cut, except that under STRATUM it shall arrive at its own time when, in the step it is sent, `hopsFrom` over `tunnels.edges` gives a path of at most `MAX_HOPS` hops from its sector to the nearest care point and every hop succeeds (one draw per hop, all drawn, in request order; a hop succeeds when its draw is below `P_HOP`), reach being decided once, at send.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-007** [10]: Under the Directorate, `p10` shall keep its `COURIERS` couriers at node 0; under STRATUM, courier 0 at node 0 and, from each step with `t mod 86400 = 0`, `t = 0` included, couriers 1 to `POPUP` at the pop-up points at node `(level − 1) × 4 + 1` of the winning levels of `rotate(POPUP, claims, rng)`, assigned in rising level order, with claims `L1` to `L6` (floor 0, demand 1, debt the requests from that level that arrived at the service in the previous 24 h), a busy courier moving when its busy time ends.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-008** [10]: In each step, `p10` shall take arrived, unserved requests in order of arrival (then request time, then draw order), with RED before AMBER under STRATUM, and give each the free courier with the fewest passable hops from its point (`hopsFrom`), the lower courier index on a tie, setting first care at `max(t, arrival) + firstCareMin(hops) × 60` and the courier busy until `max(t, arrival) + busyMin(hops) × 60`, and leave waiting any request no free courier can reach.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-009** [10]: `p10`'s metric shall be named "median hours to first care (RED)", unit `h`: the median (upper middle on an even count) over RED requests with time below `world.t` of the earlier of first care and `world.t`, minus request time (for a child not yet reached, the wait so far), in hours, and 0 when there is none.
- [x] **ADAPT-SPACECARE-010** [6, 10]: `p06` shall declare reads `sectors`, `households` and writes `sectors`, `households`; `p10` shall declare reads `tunnels` and no writes.
