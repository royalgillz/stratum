# Evidence adapter specs

Prefix `ADAPT-EVIDENCE`. Design: `evidence-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. `L<level>` names a level, for example `L5`. Day `d` is `floor(t / 86400)`; the case window at 00:00 of day `d` is days `d − CASE_WINDOW` to `d − 1` inclusive. `n1_cum`, `n2_cum` and `m_cum` are the list counts summed over all sectors since `t = 0`.

- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-001** [4]: On each step, `p04` shall draw each level's outlet quality with `outlet(level, t, events, rng)` in level order and set each sector's `water` to its level's outlet quality from `tapDelay(sector)` seconds earlier, or to the baseline while the run is younger than that delay.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-002** [4]: Under STRATUM, `p04` shall push each level's four outlet channels every step into its own `createDetector()` as `push('water.<channel>', 'L<level>', t, value)`, apply a detector hold to a level from an event's `tDetect + NOTIFY` until every event on that level has a `tEnd`, plus the longest tap delay, and treat a level as on hold when the detector hold or the official hold of ADAPT-EVIDENCE-003 applies.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-003** [4]: Under both policies, `p04` shall sample each level's outlet in the step that holds 06:00 each day, publish the result 24 h later, put the level on official hold when a published result is `unsafe`, and lift that hold when a later published result is not.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-004** [4]: `p04` shall make one `createWitness({ now: () => world.t, ledger: world.ledger })` on its first step, observe each published grab result as `('water.toc', 'L<level>', sample organic carbon, 'official')` under both policies and, under STRATUM only, each level's outlet organic carbon every whole hour as `('water.toc', 'L<level>', value, 'sensor:L<level>')`, and write no other ledger entry.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-005** [4]: `p04`'s metric shall be named "person-hours of unsafe water drunk", unit `person-h`, adding each step the people of every sector whose tap water is `unsafe` and whose level is not on hold, times `dt / 3600`.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-006** [9]: On each step `p09` shall, in this order, `seed` the sector of every outbreak event due in `[t, t + dt)`; hand out medicine when `t mod 86400 = 0`, `t = 0` included; run `epiStep(state, t, co2, stock, 3600, rng)` when `t mod 3600 = 0` and reduce each `stock` by its courses used; and write its daily ledger entries when `t mod 86400 = 0`.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-007** [9]: Under the Directorate, at 00:00 each day `p09` shall split `MED_SUPPLY` plus held-over courses in proportion to each sector's clinic-list cases in the case window, holding every course over when there are none.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-008** [9]: Under STRATUM, at 00:00 each day `p09` shall compute `N = chapman(n1, n2, m).n` from the case window's district totals, give each sector demand `N × n2_s / n2_district` (using the union `n1 + n2 − m` in place of `n2` when `n2_district` is 0, and 0 when the union is empty too), split `MED_SUPPLY` plus held-over courses by `allocate(...).shares` with floor 0, and hold the surplus over.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-009** [9]: `p09` shall write each day's medicine shares to `alloc.medicine` and add them to each sector's `stock`.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-010** [9]: `p09` shall make one `createWitness({ now: () => world.t, ledger: world.ledger })` on its first step and observe once a day `('cases', 'district', n1_cum, 'official')` under both policies and, under STRATUM only, `('cases', 'district', chapman(n1_cum, n2_cum, m_cum).n, 'estimate:capture-recapture')` and `('cases.both', 'district', m_cum, 'estimate:capture-recapture')`.
- [ ] **ADAPT-EVIDENCE-011** [9]: `p09`'s metric shall be named "case-count relative error", unit `ratio`, value `|figure − true| / max(1, true)` at the current step, with `true` the cumulative new infectious cases from `epiStep` (seeded cases excluded) and `figure` equal to `n1_cum` under the Directorate or `chapman(n1_cum, n2_cum, m_cum).n` under STRATUM.
