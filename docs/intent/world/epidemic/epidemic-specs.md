# Epidemic model specs

Prefix `WORLD-EPI`. Design: `epidemic-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. Rates are per day; `t` and `dt` are in seconds, so each probability uses `dt / 86400`. Every draw in a step uses the compartments at the start of that step.

- [ ] **WORLD-EPI-001** [9]: `createEpidemic(people)` shall give each of the 24 sectors `S` equal to its people and `E`, `I`, `R` and every list count 0, and `seed(state, sector, cases)` shall move `min(cases, S)` people of that sector from `S` to `I` without adding them to any list or to new cases.
- [ ] **WORLD-EPI-002** [9]: `epiStep` shall use the force of infection `λ = β × ((1 − MIX) × I_s / N_s + MIX × I_level / N_level)`, with `I_level` and `N_level` summed over the level's four sectors including `s`, `β = R0 × γ × min(BETA_CAP, r(co2_s) / r(CO2_REF))`, `r(c) = (c − C_OUT) / C_EXHALED` and `CO2_REF` from the air model, and no term between levels.
- [ ] **WORLD-EPI-003** [9]: `epiStep(state, t, co2, stock, dt, rng)` shall, for sectors 0 to 23 in order, draw new exposed `Bin(S, 1 − e^(−λ dt / 86400))`, new infectious `Bin(E, 1 − e^(−σ dt / 86400))`, treated recoveries `Bin(T, 1 − e^(−γT dt / 86400))` and untreated recoveries `Bin(I − T, 1 − e^(−γ dt / 86400))` with `T = min(I, floor(stock_s))`, then the list draws of WORLD-EPI-005, and report per sector the new infectious cases and the treated recoveries as courses used.
- [ ] **WORLD-EPI-004** [9]: `epiStep` shall keep every compartment a non-negative integer and `S + E + I + R` equal to the sector's people.
- [ ] **WORLD-EPI-005** [9]: For `k` new infectious cases in a sector, `epiStep` shall draw `n1 = Bin(k, CLIN_TOP − CLIN_STEP × (level − 1))`, then `m = Bin(n1, P_REPORT)`, then `n2 = m + Bin(k − n1, P_REPORT)`, and add them to that sector's clinic, residents' and both counts for day `floor(t / 86400)`, so every step makes exactly seven `binomial` calls per sector.
- [ ] **WORLD-EPI-006** [9]: `binomial(n, p, rng)` shall draw exactly one `u = rng()` and return the smallest `k` whose CDF exceeds `u` (or `n` when none does), return `n − binomial(n, 1 − p)` from the same `u` when `p > 0.5`, return 0 for `p = 0` and `n` for `p = 1`; over 100,000 draws from `rng(1, 'test')`, `Bin(50, 0.3)` and `Bin(50, 0.8)` shall each have a sample mean within 0.05 of `np` and a sample variance within 2% of `np(1 − p)`.
- [ ] **WORLD-EPI-007** [9]: Given the same people, seeds, `t`, CO2, stock, `dt` and `rng` sequence, the epidemic functions shall return identical states.
- [ ] **WORLD-EPI-008** [9]: The epidemic functions shall throw a RangeError for a sector outside 0 to 23, negative or non-integer cases, a negative or non-finite stock, a CO2 below `C_OUT` or not finite, a non-finite or negative `t`, `p` outside [0, 1], or a non-positive `dt`.
