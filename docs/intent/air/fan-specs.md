# Air watch (fan stop) specs

Prefix `AIR-FAN`. Design: `fan-design.md`. The problems served are in brackets. A "reading" is one accepted `push(t, level)`, `t` in seconds and `level` in dB. "The window" is the accepted readings with time in `(t − 20, t]`. A condition is "held for d s" when it is true on every reading from the first of an unbroken run to the current one, and the current `t` minus that first `t` is at least d; every run restarts when the state changes and when two accepted readings are more than 2 s apart. "The last 3 s" is the window's readings with time in `(t − 3, t]`.

- [x] **AIR-FAN-001** [1]: `createFanWatch({ label })` shall throw RangeError for a label other than LIVE, REPLAY or SIMULATED, shall start in state LISTENING, and the watch shall read no clock and use no randomness.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-002** [1]: If `push` is given a `t` that is not finite or is earlier than the last accepted `t`, then the watch shall throw RangeError and change nothing; if it is given a `level` that is not finite, then it shall return null and change nothing.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-003** [1]: On each reading, the watch shall set the baseline `B` to the window's 20th percentile (sorted levels, index `floor(0.2 × (n − 1))`) and call the reading steady when the window's median (index `floor(0.5 × (n − 1))`) is at most 4 dB above `B`.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-004** [1]: The watch shall keep the quiet floor `F` as the lowest `B` of any reading, never above −100 dB.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-005** [1]: While LISTENING or STOPPED, when readings have been steady with `B ≥ F + 10 dB` held for 10 s, the watch shall enter RUNNING.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-006** [1]: While RUNNING (including the reading that enters it), when the window's oldest reading is at least 10 s old, the watch shall set the running level `R` to the highest `B` among steady readings in `(t − 10, t]`, keeping the previous `R` when there is none or the window is younger; while STOPPED it shall keep `R` unchanged.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-007** [1]: While RUNNING, when, among the last 3 s, the readings with `level ≤ R − 6 dB` are at least 70% of those with `level ≤ R + 3 dB` and at least 20% of all, the watch shall enter STOPPED; it shall never enter STOPPED from LISTENING.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-008** [1]: While STOPPED, when `level > R − 6 dB` has held for 5 s, the watch shall enter RUNNING, drop the window's readings from before that run and forget every earlier steady reading, so `B` and `R` follow the fan that came back, not the quiet before it.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-009** [1]: Each reading shall carry `t`, `state`, `levelDb`, `baselineDb` (`B`), `floorDb` (`F`), `runningDb` (`R`, null before the first RUNNING), `steady` and the creation label.
- [x] **AIR-FAN-010** [1]: Given 10 readings a second of a fan with speech bursts for 5 minutes, the watch shall reach RUNNING and never report STOPPED, and when that fan then stops, with or without the speech going on, or spins down over 10 s, it shall report STOPPED within 5 s (spike T4 pass mark).
- [x] **AIR-FAN-011** [1]: When readings resume after a gap of more than 2 s (a frozen or throttled tab), the watch shall restart every run and keep `R` until the window again spans 10 s, so a fan that stopped during the gap is reported STOPPED by the readings after it, and readings once a minute never make a fan RUNNING.
