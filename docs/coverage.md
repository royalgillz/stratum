## Ablations

SIMULATED. `npm run harness:ablations -- --seeds 20` (`public/evidence/ablations.json`): the seven-day week, seeds 1 to 20, five conditions. An engine is switched off by running its adapters under the Directorate's rule and the rest under STRATUM's. Mapping (`docs/intent/adapters/adapters-design.md`, "Ablations"): Witness p01, p04, p09, p13, p14; Mesh p07, p08, p10; Fair Share p02, p03, p05, p06, p11, p12, p15. The STRATUM and Directorate conditions match `seven-day.json` seed for seed. A contribution is STRATUM minus STRATUM without the engine, paired by seed, with a 95% interval (in the file).

| Engine off | What changes (STRATUM, then STRATUM without the engine) |
| --- | --- |
| Witness | cuts warned 10 min ahead 0.40 to 0.00; unsafe water drunk 0 to 23,280 person-hours; case-count relative error 0.20 to 0.79 (contribution -0.59, 95%: -0.68 to -0.49); wrong-band rate 0.00 to 1.00; protection factor proven 10 to 10,000 |
| Mesh | messages delivered within 60 min 0.48 to 0.00; mean hours until status known 2.17 to 3.92; median hours to first care (RED) 0.58 to 17.1 |
| Fair Share | worst-decile served 0.85 to 0.66; heat 9,336 to 64,904 person-hours; stranded 0 to 6,082 person-hours; sleeping m2 per sleeper 5.39 to 2.00; daylight share 0.64 to 0.19; food floor held 5.55 to 3.00 days; worst level's wait 14.4 to 22.8 days; cuts warned 0.40 to 0.15 |

What goes against us:

- The engines barely interact. Almost every metric moves only when its own adapters switch, so the ablation shows each adapter's rule, not a loop worth more than its parts. The two cross effects are below.
- Without Fair Share, cuts warned 10 min ahead fall from 0.40 to 0.15 (contribution 0.25, 95%: 0.245 to 0.250). Most of p01's gain comes from which sectors Fair Share cuts and when, not from the hum warning alone.
- Without Fair Share, the case-count error is 0.16 against STRATUM's 0.20 (contribution 0.04, 95%: -0.06 to 0.13): no gain from Fair Share there, and the seven-day outbreak is small (see Sweeps).
- Several Directorate figures are set by its stated rule (adapters-design, "What sets a number"): p01 counts no warnings, p13 and p14 keep a fixed verdict, p07, p08 and p10 wait for the 06:00 bulletin. Switching Witness or Mesh off returns those values, so those contributions show the mechanism, not a measured gain.

What goes against us:

- From 3 lying crews in 5 the quorum carries the wrong band: STRATUM's wrong-band rate goes from 0.00 to 1.00, the same as the Directorate's fixed verdict. The staged trial still proves factor 10, at the cost of 1 breach and 8 more person-hours.
- The deeper the shortfall, the less the hum warns: from 0.5 of capacity, cuts warned fall from 0.40 to 0.20. Heat under STRATUM rises from 0 to 42,009 person-hours at 0.7, still below the Directorate at every point.
- Small outbreaks give a poor case estimate: error 0.30 (95%: 0.20 to 0.40) at 2 cases a sector and 0.36 (95%: 0.14 to 0.57) at 5. It falls to 0.11 (95%: 0.07 to 0.15) at 40.
- Grid inertia moves nothing: every figure is identical from H = 1 s to 9 s. The model steps every 60 s and its time constant 2H/D is 1 to 9 s, so the frequency settles inside one step whatever the inertia. The sweep cannot show the first seconds after a trip, where inertia matters; it shows only that the warning result does not hang on the assumed H.
- Capture-recapture needs reports: at a quarter of the model's capture STRATUM's case-count error is 0.66 (95%: 0.57 to 0.74), against the Directorate's 0.95. STRATUM is better at every point. At 0.75 one seed (20) overestimates by 2.80, the few-on-both-lists failure in `docs/limits.md`, which widens that point's interval to 0.08 to 0.64.
